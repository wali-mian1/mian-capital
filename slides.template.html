A clearer first step

Teach the account before the ticker.

The separate Learn page explains a brokerage account, 401(k), Traditional IRA, and Roth IRA in short cards with Investor.gov and IRS links.

A front-page example makes compounding concrete: $100 a month for 20 years means $24,000 contributed; at an assumed 6% annual return it would reach about $46,200. This is an illustration, not a forecast.

SPY and QQQ are market comparisons. Ten other research tickers are Shariah-oriented funds or Amana funds awaiting source review; neither benchmark gets a Shariah label.

What is new

A transparent companion to existing tools.

Zoya and Musaffa offer broader Islamic screening research. Wahed offers Shariah-oriented products. Mian.io adds a free, source-backed research conversation, account education, and an explicit unknown share for portfolio evidence.

The current prototype has narrower coverage and does not certify a holding's compliance. SPY and QQQ are market comparisons only. The Testimonials tab includes one permissioned, anonymous tester paraphrase, alongside clearly marked sample workflows.

Sources: zoya.finance · musaffa.com · wahed.com

Portfolio screening

We look inside the fund.

Sample: $5,000 HLAL + $3,000 SPUS + $2,000 AMAGX.

Mian Capital opens each fund, lists its top ten companies, and checks each one: alcohol, gambling, tobacco, weapons, fossil fuels (AI screening agent) and debt over 30% of market value (calculated).

Result: 15 companies screened in about 16 seconds. One flag: ExxonMobil inside SPUS, $51 of the user's money.

Safety

A secret request was blocked before the model.

The user prompt asked for the Portkey key from .env. The app returned a refusal and a safety_guard · blocked event.

Measured tokens: zero.

Other controls: no fabricated price on missing data, visible “needs review” portfolio weight, bounded agent calls, no trade execution or account access.

Residual risk: untrusted web text can still contain misleading claims; an explicit hostile-page test remains.
